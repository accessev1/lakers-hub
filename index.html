How it turned

The Los AngelesLakers opened the post-LeBron era with a statement win, pulling away from the Golden StateWarriors at home. Walker Kessler authored a historic debut: a perfect 12-for-12 shooting night with 10 dunks — the first Los AngelesLakers player with a perfect game on 12 or more attempts since Wilt Chamberlain in 1969, and the most dunks by a Laker since Shaquille O’Neal in 2003. Luka Doncic traded trash talk with Golden StateWarriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg and buried a half-court buzzer-beater, and the crowd’s “We want Bronny” chants were rewarded when Bronny James checked in and hit a floater.

Key lines

  • Luka Doncic: 28 points, 8 rebounds, 7 assists and 3 steals, plus the half-court buzzer-beater.
  • Walker Kessler: 26 points on a perfect 12-for-12 from the field (10 dunks), 9 rebounds and 2 blocks — while playing with a splint on his left finger.
  • Austin Reaves: 23 points, 6 assists and 4 rebounds.
  • Bronny James: Answered the crowd’s chants with a floater in his first regular-season action of the year.

Record book: Shaquille O’Neal dubbed Kessler “Milk Chamberlain” after the debut and called him the best big man in the NBA.

News & Rumors

Updated Thursday, October 22 • Newest first

Kessler’s historic debut: “Milk Chamberlain”

Walker Kessler scored 26 points on a perfect 12-for-12 shooting night in his Los AngelesLakers debut — the first Laker with a perfect game on 12 or more attempts since Wilt Chamberlain in 1969. His 10 dunks were the most by a Laker since Shaquille O’Neal in 2003, and Shaq himself dubbed him “Milk Chamberlain” and called him the best big man in the NBA. Kessler did it all with a splint on his left finger.

Doncic wins the opener — and the trash-talk duel

Luka Doncic went back and forth with Golden StateWarriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg all night and finished with 28 points, 8 rebounds, 7 assists and 3 steals, capping it with a half-court buzzer-beater in the 127–105 win.

Williams and Thybulle win the final starting spots

Ziaire Williams and Matisse Thybulle earned the last two spots in the starting five for the opener, beating out Quentin Grimes. The first five: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler.

“We want Bronny” — and Bronny delivers

The Crypto.com Arena crowd chanted for Bronny James late in the blowout win, and he answered with a floater after checking in.

Lakers waive Dalton Knecht to set the opening-night roster

The Los AngelesLakers waived Dalton Knecht on Monday, October 19 to reach the 15-man roster limit, and the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy both made the opening-night roster.

Suns extend center Oso Ighodaro

The PhoenixSuns agreed to a three-year, $29 million extension with center Oso Ighodaro.

Reaves: chemistry with new teammates will take time

Austin Reaves admitted building chemistry with the new teammates — especially Walker Kessler — will take time after a preseason in which the big three shared the floor for just one half, per Yahoo Sports (October 19). One game in, the early returns are promising: Reaves opened with 23 points and 6 assists.

Doncic enters the season as the focal point — health is the question

Luka Doncic comes in as the centerpiece after winning his second scoring title at 33.5 points per game last season, and he arrives lean after a full offseason of conditioning. The big question is health: he missed last season’s playoff run with a Grade 2 hamstring strain, per USA Today (October 19).

Mamukelashvili’s preseason, by the numbers

Sandro Mamukelashvili averaged 13.4 points, 4.2 rebounds and 3.4 assists across the preseason, shooting 55.6% from the field and 54.2% from three. JJ Redick praised his quick decision-making.
