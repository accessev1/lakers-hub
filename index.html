How it went: The first quarter was tied 28–28. The Los AngelesLakers then won the second quarter 33–26 and the third 26–20, and the fourth quarter was tied 29–29, for a 116–103 road win and a third straight victory.

Top performers

  • Luka Doncic: 40 points on 11 of 23 shooting, 6 of 13 from three point range and 12 of 13 at the free throw line, plus 9 rebounds and 7 assists.
  • Austin Reaves: 21 points and 4 assists.
  • Walker Kessler: 15 points and 4 blocks.

For the SacramentoKings: Darius Acuff Jr. had 22 points and 12 assists; Domantas Sabonis had 16 points and 17 rebounds; Zach LaVine scored 21 points.

Doncic milestones: This was his third straight 40 point game and his fifth of the season. He leads the league in scoring at 38.5 points per game, and his 308 points through 8 games are the most at that point of a season since Michael Jordan scored 303 in 1988–89. He has also put up at least 35 points, 5 rebounds, 5 assists and 5 three pointers in six straight games, the longest run of its kind in NBA history.
