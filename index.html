By quarter: UtahJazz 29–27–22–31; Los AngelesLakers 34–20–22–21.

How it went: The game was tied 83–83 with 8:19 left, then Utah ripped off a 15–0 fourth-quarter run to pull away for a 109–97 win — snapping the Los AngelesLakers' nine-game winning streak and ending their unbeaten November. The Lakers shot season lows of 38.2 percent from the field and 27.3 percent from three, and their 18 turnovers turned into 26 Utah points.

Top performers

  • Luka Doncic: 29 points on 10 of 28 from the floor and 2 of 12 from three, with 5 rebounds, 7 assists and 9 turnovers — ending his streak of 11 straight 30-point games.
  • Quentin Grimes: 20 points.
  • Walker Kessler: Returned with 10 points and 11 rebounds.
  • Keyonte George (Utah): 30 points, 10 assists and 5 three-pointers.
  • Ace Bailey (Utah): 19 points and 8 rebounds.
  • Mo Bamba (Utah): 16 rebounds.

The takeaway: The streak is over at nine, but the Lakers stay 13–3 and alone atop the West. Doncic called his own play "unacceptable" afterward, and the bounce-back chance comes Friday at home against a 1–13 Sacramento team.

News & Rumors

Updated Thursday, November 19 • Newest first

Doncic calls his own play "unacceptable"

Luka Doncic took the blame for Wednesday's 109–97 loss to the UtahJazz, calling his play "unacceptable" after committing nine turnovers and shooting 2 of 12 from three. His 29 points ended his streak of 11 straight 30-point games.

Last Two Minute Report: Doncic traveled before the Grimes three

The NBA's Last Two Minute Report says Doncic traveled before the pass that set up Quentin Grimes' game-tying three with 1.7 seconds left against the CharlotteHornets on November 17 — and that a foul should have been called on Doncic earlier in the sequence instead of a jump ball. The Los AngelesLakers won that game 118–114 in overtime.

Redick: Reaves' absence is taking a toll on Doncic

Coach JJ Redick said Austin Reaves' absence with a bruised left heel is taking a toll on Doncic, leading to back-to-back inefficient nights — while adding that fatigue is no excuse for the team's 38 percent shooting against Utah.

Hot mic catches Doncic arguing with officials

A hot mic caught Doncic arguing with referee Tre Maddox at halftime of the Utah game and trading words with official Dannica Baroody in the third quarter.

Rumor: Max Christie would welcome a Lakers return — no interest shown

NBA insider Jovan Buha reported November 14 that ex-Laker Max Christie was "sad" and "really bummed out" after being traded to Dallas for Doncic and would welcome a return to the Los AngelesLakers, but the Lakers have shown no interest in bringing him back. There is no trade request and no official Lakers interest.

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.

Rafael Castro and Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones waived (October 17)
