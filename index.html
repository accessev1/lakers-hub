Last Game Recap

Final • Friday, November 27 • Frost Bank Center, San Antonio • NBA Cup West Group C finale • Los AngelesLakers now 15–4 • First in the Pacific Division • Eliminated from the NBA Cup at 2–2 in group play

San AntonioSpurs
Win
99–96
Los AngelesLakers

How it went: The Los AngelesLakers led by as many as 20 points and took a 54–38 lead into halftime, but the San AntonioSpurs rallied in the second half to win the NBA Cup West Group C finale 99–96. The loss eliminated the Lakers from the NBA Cup at 2–2 in group play.

Top performers

  • Luka Doncic: 31 points, 8 assists and 6 rebounds — 14 of 31 from the field and 1 of 13 from three.
  • Austin Reaves: 21 points and 8 rebounds in his return after missing five games with a bruised left heel.
  • Walker Kessler: 10 points, 13 rebounds and 4 assists.
  • Collin Sexton: 12 points.
  • Stephon Castle (San Antonio): 26 points and 9 assists.
  • Victor Wembanyama (San Antonio): 25 points, 16 rebounds, 4 steals and 2 blocks.
  • Dylan Harper (San Antonio): 14 points.
  • De'Aaron Fox (San Antonio): 12 points.

Team stats: The Lakers committed 17 turnovers, and the giveaways helped fuel San Antonio's comeback from 20 down.

The takeaway: A blown 20-point lead costs the Lakers their NBA Cup run and sole possession of the West's best record — San Antonio now sits alone on top at 16–4, with the Lakers a half game back at 15–4 heading to Memphis on Sunday.

Business item: Kalshi partnership announced (Tuesday, November 24)

The Los AngelesLakers announced a partnership with Kalshi as the team's official prediction market sponsor. This is a business item, not a player transaction.

Trade ideas, not reported talks: Lakers insider Jovan Buha floated packaging Jaden Hardy with three second-round picks for BrooklynNets center Day'Ron Sharpe or SacramentoKings big man Precious Achiuwa; Brook Lopez of the Los AngelesClippers was also named as a fit. These are ideas only, not reported talks. The Los AngelesLakers are also fans of MilwaukeeBucks center Jericho Sims, per insider Jake Fischer. No deal has been made. The trade deadline is February 11, 2027.

The rebuild that set this roster

Austin Reaves was re-signed for four years and $184.8 million, and Walker Kessler arrived via trade to anchor the middle. New signings Collin Sexton, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Jaden Hardy round out the rotation. The Los AngelesLakers have opened the season at 15–4, and Jarred Vanderbilt made his season debut on Wednesday, collecting 7 rebounds in 21 minutes at the Los AngelesClippers.

Starting five through three games

JJ Redick’s first five: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler. Williams and Thybulle won the final two starting spots over Quentin Grimes, and all three scored in double figures on Friday, with Williams scoring 14 and Thybulle and Grimes 13 each.

Dalton Knecht waived (October 19)

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.

Rafael Castro and Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones waived (October 17)

The Los AngelesLakers waived both players Saturday, per ESPN’s Dave McMenamin. Tait-Jones had just signed an Exhibit 10 deal as a G League pipeline move.

Cole Swider and Jahmir Young waived earlier this week

The Los AngelesLakers opened the week by waiving Swider and Young as the camp roster took shape.
