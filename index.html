Roster watch • Updated Saturday, October 31

No new Los AngelesLakers transaction reported today. The most recent move came Tuesday, October 27, when the Los AngelesLakers recalled Cameron Carr from the Coachella Valley Lakers (G League). Carr remains out with a right oblique strain.

The rebuild that set this roster

Austin Reaves was re-signed for four years and $184.8 million, and Walker Kessler arrived via trade to anchor the middle. New signings Collin Sexton, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Jaden Hardy round out the rotation, while LeBron James departed for the Philadelphia76ers. The first Los AngelesLakers season of the post-LeBron era has opened at 4–2, and Jarred Vanderbilt made his season debut on Wednesday, collecting 7 rebounds in 21 minutes at the Los AngelesClippers.

Starting five through three games

JJ Redick’s first five: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler. Williams and Thybulle won the final two starting spots over Quentin Grimes, and all three scored in double figures on Friday, with Williams scoring 14 and Thybulle and Grimes 13 each.

Dalton Knecht waived (October 19)

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.

Rafael Castro and Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones waived (October 17)

The Los AngelesLakers waived both players Saturday, per ESPN’s Dave McMenamin. Tait-Jones had just signed an Exhibit 10 deal as a G League pipeline move.

Cole Swider and Jahmir Young waived earlier this week

The Los AngelesLakers opened the week by waiving Swider and Young as the camp roster took shape.
