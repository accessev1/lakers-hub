How it turned

Luka Doncic had a rough night, so Austin Reaves took over. Reaves scored 13 of his 28 points in the third quarter, and the Los AngelesLakers put up 39 points in that quarter while making 72.7 percent of their shots. The Los AngelesLakers made 58.2 percent of their shots for the game, and their defense forced the Los AngelesClippers into 26 turnovers, which the Los AngelesLakers turned into 33 points. Walker Kessler set the tone early with a first-quarter steal and dunk on his way to 22 points, and the Los AngelesLakers also paused in the first quarter to honor former forward Rui Hachimura, now with the Los AngelesClippers, with a tribute video in his return to Crypto.com Arena.

Roster watch • Updated Sunday, October 25

No new transactions today. The roster stands as set for today’s road game against the UtahJazz.

The rebuild that set this roster

Austin Reaves was re-signed for four years and $184.8 million, and Walker Kessler arrived via trade to anchor the middle. New signings Collin Sexton, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Jaden Hardy round out the rotation, while LeBron James departed for the Philadelphia76ers. The first Los AngelesLakers season of the post-LeBron era has opened with two straight wins.

Starting five through two games

JJ Redick’s first five: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler. Williams and Thybulle won the final two starting spots over Quentin Grimes, and all three scored in double figures on Friday, with Williams scoring 14 and Thybulle and Grimes 13 each.

Dalton Knecht waived (October 19)

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.

Rafael Castro and Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones waived (October 17)

The Los AngelesLakers waived both players Saturday, per ESPN’s Dave McMenamin. Tait-Jones had just signed an Exhibit 10 deal as a G League pipeline move.

Cole Swider and Jahmir Young waived earlier this week

The Los AngelesLakers opened the week by waiving Swider and Young as the camp roster took shape.
