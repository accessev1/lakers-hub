By quarter: Los AngelesLakers 33, 31, 40, 27 • PhoenixSuns 24, 30, 26, 43.

How it went: The Los AngelesLakers never trailed, led 23–5 early and by 31 late in the third quarter, then held on for a 131–123 win at the PhoenixSuns to finish a 4–0 road trip. Phoenix won the fourth quarter 43–27 behind Collin Gillespie (26 points) to get within five with 1:05 left. Devin Booker had 17 points on 4 of 14 shooting and did not play in the fourth quarter.

Top performers

  • Luka Doncic: 43 points, 9 assists and 5 three-pointers on 13 of 28 from the floor and 12 of 14 at the line — his sixth 40-point game this season.
  • Quentin Grimes: 22 points and 6 rebounds on 7 of 10 from the floor and 5 of 6 from three, starting for the injured Austin Reaves, including a banked three with 34 seconds left.
  • Collin Gillespie (Phoenix): 26 points, leading the Suns' fourth-quarter rally.
  • Devin Booker (Phoenix): 17 points on 4 of 14 shooting; did not play in the fourth quarter.

The takeaway: Eight straight wins and 12–2 to close a perfect 4–0 road trip, but the Lakers nearly blew a 31-point lead late — the late-game help around Doncic remains the watch item heading into tonight vs Charlotte.
