How it turned

Luka Doncic authored the first 50-point game of the NBA season, pouring in 52 points on 17-for-30 shooting with 8 three-pointers, 10 assists and 8 rebounds — the first game in Los AngelesLakers history with at least 50 points, 10 assists, 5 rebounds and 5 three-pointers. Austin Reaves added 29 points as the Los AngelesLakers held off a UtahJazz team that got 28 points from Lauri Markkanen and a near triple-double from Keyonte George. Walker Kessler had a quiet night in his return to Utah, finishing with 2 points and 7 rebounds.

Top performers

  • Luka Doncic: 52 points, 10 assists and 8 rebounds, with 8 three-pointers on 17-for-30 shooting.
  • Austin Reaves: 29 points.
  • Walker Kessler: 2 points and 7 rebounds in his return to Utah.
  • Lauri Markkanen (UtahJazz): 28 points.
  • Keyonte George (UtahJazz): 24 points, 10 assists and 9 rebounds.
  • Jaren Jackson Jr. (UtahJazz): 20 points.
  • Ace Bailey (UtahJazz): 17 points and 12 rebounds.

Team notes: The win moves the Los AngelesLakers to 3–0, alone on top of the Pacific Division, and makes them one of six unbeaten teams after the season’s first week. Doncic’s 52 points are a season high across the league so far.

The rebuild that set this roster

Austin Reaves was re-signed for four years and $184.8 million, and Walker Kessler arrived via trade to anchor the middle. New signings Collin Sexton, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Jaden Hardy round out the rotation, while LeBron James departed for the Philadelphia76ers. The first Los AngelesLakers season of the post-LeBron era has opened with three straight wins.

Starting five through three games

JJ Redick’s first five: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler. Williams and Thybulle won the final two starting spots over Quentin Grimes, and all three scored in double figures on Friday, with Williams scoring 14 and Thybulle and Grimes 13 each.

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.
