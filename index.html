Final • Friday, November 6 • Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles • Los AngelesLakers now 8–2 • NBA Cup group play: 1–1

Los AngelesLakers
Win
124–118
PortlandTrail Blazers
Loss

How it went: The Los AngelesLakers lost the first quarter 29–20 and trailed 60–53 at halftime, then outscored the PortlandTrail Blazers 34–15 in the third quarter to take control. Portland won the fourth 43–37, but the rally held for a 124–118 win, a fourth straight victory, and revenge for the 136–129 loss at Portland on October 27. Quarter scores: Los AngelesLakers 20–33–34–37, PortlandTrail Blazers 29–31–15–43.

Top performers

  • Luka Doncic: 53 points, 5 rebounds and 5 assists on 17 of 33 shooting, 5 of 15 from three point range and 14 of 18 at the free throw line — including 20 points in the third quarter and a buzzer-beating heave from beyond half court to end the first half.
  • Austin Reaves: 25 points and 4 steals.
  • Walker Kessler: 16 points and 12 rebounds.
  • Adou Thiero: 4 points, 4 rebounds and 2 assists in 6:37 in his season debut.
  • Sandro Mamukelashvili: 7 points in 20 minutes after his illness.

For the PortlandTrail Blazers: Deni Avdija had 22 points, 9 rebounds and 11 assists; Damian Lillard scored 22 points.

Doncic milestones: Doncic has scored at least 40 points in four straight games, the first Laker to do that since Kobe Bryant in 2012. This was his 11th career 50 point game, tying him for 10th on the NBA all-time list.
