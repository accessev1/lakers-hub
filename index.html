Final • Sunday, November 8 • Little Caesars Arena, Detroit • Los AngelesLakers now 9–2 • Five straight wins • 2–0 this season without Luka Doncic • Halftime: Los AngelesLakers 61, DetroitPistons 48

Los AngelesLakers
Win
121–116
DetroitPistons
Loss

How it went: The Los AngelesLakers led 38–14 after the first quarter and by 26 early in the second, forcing eight DetroitPistons turnovers and scoring 17 points off them. Detroit got within one point three times in the fourth quarter. Austin Reaves hit a banked three with 6:41 left in the third quarter to make it 78–61, then an off-balance one-legged three with 44.5 seconds left to make it 112–104, and closed it out at the free throw line. Quarter scores: Los AngelesLakers 38–23–27–33, DetroitPistons 14–34–33–35.

Top performers

  • Austin Reaves: 43 points — a season high and his first 40-point game of the season — with 5 rebounds and 4 assists on 14 of 27 shooting from the floor, 4 of 6 from three point range and 11 of 11 at the free throw line.
  • Ziaire Williams: 17 points off the bench.
  • Collin Sexton: 17 points off the bench.
  • Walker Kessler: 12 points and 16 rebounds.

For the DetroitPistons: Daniss Jenkins scored 31 points with 8 three-pointers.

Doncic out: Luka Doncic missed the game with lower back tightness, his second absence of the season. The Los AngelesLakers are 2–0 without him this season.
