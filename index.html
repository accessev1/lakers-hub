How it turned

The Los AngelesLakers opened the post-LeBron era with a statement win, pulling away from the Golden StateWarriors at home. Walker Kessler authored a historic debut: a perfect 12-for-12 shooting night with 10 dunks — the most by a Laker since Shaquille O’Neal in 2003, and only the second perfect game on 12 or more attempts in Los AngelesLakers history, after Wilt Chamberlain in 1969. Luka Doncic traded trash talk with Golden StateWarriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg — who took a technical foul — and buried a half-court buzzer-beater to close the first half. The Los AngelesLakers then won the third quarter 36–16 to blow the game open, and the crowd’s “We want Bronny” chants were rewarded when Bronny James checked in late and hit a floater.

Key lines

  • Luka Doncic: 28 points, 8 rebounds, 7 assists and 3 steals, plus the half-court buzzer-beater.
  • Walker Kessler: 26 points on a perfect 12-for-12 from the field (10 dunks), 9 rebounds and 2 blocks — while playing with a splint on his left finger.
  • Austin Reaves: 23 points, 6 assists and 4 rebounds.
  • Stephen Curry: 23 points for the Golden StateWarriors.
  • Bronny James: Answered the crowd’s chants with a floater in his first regular-season action of the year.

Record book: Shaquille O’Neal dubbed Kessler “Milk Chamberlain” after the debut and called him the best big man in the NBA.

No new transactions today — the roster stands as set for tonight’s game against the Los AngelesClippers.

The rebuild that set this roster

Austin Reaves was re-signed for four years and $184.8 million, and Walker Kessler arrived via trade to anchor the middle. New signings Collin Sexton, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Jaden Hardy round out the rotation, while LeBron James departed for the Philadelphia76ers — the first Los AngelesLakers season of the post-LeBron era opened with Wednesday’s 127–105 win.

Starting five settled for the opener

JJ Redick’s first five on opening night: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler — Williams and Thybulle won the final two starting spots over Quentin Grimes.

Dalton Knecht waived (October 19)

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.

Rafael Castro and Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones waived (October 17)

The Los AngelesLakers waived both players Saturday, per ESPN’s Dave McMenamin. Tait-Jones had just signed an Exhibit 10 deal as a G League pipeline move.

Cole Swider and Jahmir Young waived earlier this week

The Los AngelesLakers opened the week by waiving Swider and Young as the camp roster took shape.
