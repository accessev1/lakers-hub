News & Rumors

Updated Thursday, October 29 • Newest first

Lakers win at the Clippers without Doncic

No Luka Doncic, no problem: the Los AngelesLakers beat the Los AngelesClippers 103–87 at the Intuit Dome on Wednesday behind Quentin Grimes (23 points, 5 assists, 4 steals) and Collin Sexton (21 points off the bench). A 16–0 second-quarter run and a defensive stand that held the Los AngelesClippers to 17.6 percent shooting in the quarter built a 56–38 halftime lead.

Sexton: “Whenever I step between those lines, it is wartime”

Collin Sexton’s full-court defense went viral during Wednesday’s win, and his quote summed up the approach: “Whenever I step between those lines, it is wartime.” He backed it up with 21 points off the bench on 4-of-7 shooting from three-point range.

Redick hopeful Doncic and Williams return Friday

Coach JJ Redick is hopeful Luka Doncic (back tightness) and Ziaire Williams (left foot soreness) return Friday at the Golden StateWarriors. Neither player’s status for Friday has been confirmed yet.

Doncic reaches 3,000 points as a Laker — fastest ever

Luka Doncic has reached 3,000 points as a member of the Los AngelesLakers in just 95 games, the fastest in franchise history, passing George Mikan, who needed 106 games. The milestone followed his 52-point night at the UtahJazz on October 25.

Vanderbilt makes his season debut

Jarred Vanderbilt made his season debut on Wednesday at the Los AngelesClippers, collecting 7 rebounds in 21 minutes as the Los AngelesLakers won without Doncic.

Doncic’s historic 52-point night

Luka Doncic scored 52 points with 10 assists, 8 rebounds and 8 three-pointers on 17-for-30 shooting in Sunday’s 132–123 win at the UtahJazz. It was the first 50-point game of the NBA season, and the first game in Los AngelesLakers history with at least 50 points, 10 assists, 5 rebounds and 5 three-pointers.

NBA Cup group play Friday at Golden State

The Los AngelesLakers (4–1) visit the Golden StateWarriors (2–3) on Friday in NBA Cup group play (7:00 PM Pacific, Prime Video). A win moves the Los AngelesLakers to 5–1 and keeps them alone on top of the Pacific Division.

Roster watch • Updated Thursday, October 29

Cameron Carr recalled from the G League (October 27). The Los AngelesLakers recalled Carr from the Coachella Valley Lakers on Tuesday. Carr remains out with a right oblique strain.

The rebuild that set this roster

Austin Reaves was re-signed for four years and $184.8 million, and Walker Kessler arrived via trade to anchor the middle. New signings Collin Sexton, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Jaden Hardy round out the rotation, while LeBron James departed for the Philadelphia76ers. The first Los AngelesLakers season of the post-LeBron era has opened at 4–1, and Jarred Vanderbilt made his season debut on Wednesday, collecting 7 rebounds in 21 minutes at the Los AngelesClippers.

Starting five through three games

JJ Redick’s first five: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler. Williams and Thybulle won the final two starting spots over Quentin Grimes, and all three scored in double figures on Friday, with Williams scoring 14 and Thybulle and Grimes 13 each.

Dalton Knecht waived (October 19)

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.

Rafael Castro and Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones waived (October 17)

The Los AngelesLakers waived both players Saturday, per ESPN’s Dave McMenamin. Tait-Jones had just signed an Exhibit 10 deal as a G League pipeline move.

Cole Swider and Jahmir Young waived earlier this week

The Los AngelesLakers opened the week by waiving Swider and Young as the camp roster took shape.
