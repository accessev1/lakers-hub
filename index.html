Last Game Recap

Final • Monday, November 23 • Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles • Los AngelesLakers now 15–3 • First in the Pacific Division • Tied with San Antonio for the NBA's best record • Winners of 11 of their last 12

Los AngelesLakers
Win
109–94
UtahJazz
Loss

How it went: Luka Doncic scored 14 points in the first quarter as the Los AngelesLakers built a 30–20 lead, pushed it to 50–34 late in the second quarter and led 53–42 at halftime. Utah opened the third quarter on a 15–3 run to lead 57–56 and still led 74–71 with 1:27 left in the third, but the Lakers closed to lead 78–77 entering the fourth, opened the quarter on a 17–5 run and outscored Utah 31–17 in the period — Jake LaRavia's three-pointer made it 102–88 with 3:35 left. The win avenged last Wednesday's 109–97 loss to Utah that ended the Lakers' nine-game winning streak.

Top performers

  • Luka Doncic: 28 points and 8 assists — 14 of his points came in the first quarter.
  • Jake LaRavia: A season-high 15 points and 10 rebounds.
  • Matisse Thybulle: 14 points.
  • Walker Kessler: 10 points, 12 rebounds and 2 blocks against his former team.
  • Collin Sexton: 12 points.
  • Cameron Carr: A career-high 9 points in 10 minutes.
  • Kyle Filipowski (Utah): 21 points and 12 rebounds off the bench.
  • Ace Bailey (Utah): 18 points.
  • Keyonte George (Utah): 14 points.
  • Darryn Peterson (Utah): 13 points.

Team stats: The Lakers shot 43.2 percent and 12 of 42 from three; Utah shot 41.6 percent and 6 of 32 from three. It was the Lakers' NBA-leading sixth game with at least 10 steals and 6 blocks — they are 6–0 in those games. Fast-break points went 26–9 to the Lakers and points off turnovers 20–7. Austin Reaves missed his fifth straight game.

The takeaway: Payback complete — the Lakers win for the 11th time in 12 games to reach 15–3, tied with San Antonio for the best record in the NBA ahead of Friday's showdown.

The rebuild that set this roster

Starting five through three games

JJ Redick’s first five: Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, Ziaire Williams, Matisse Thybulle and Walker Kessler. Williams and Thybulle won the final two starting spots over Quentin Grimes, and all three scored in double figures on Friday, with Williams scoring 14 and Thybulle and Grimes 13 each.

Dalton Knecht waived (October 19)

The Los AngelesLakers waived Knecht on Monday to reach the 15-man roster limit; the roster now sits at 15 standard contracts. The team eats his $4.2 million salary, and Knecht is a free agent. Bronny James and Jaden Hardy make the opening-night roster.

Rafael Castro and Aniwaniwa Tait-Jones waived (October 17)

The Los AngelesLakers waived both players Saturday, per ESPN’s Dave McMenamin. Tait-Jones had just signed an Exhibit 10 deal as a G League pipeline move.

Cole Swider and Jahmir Young waived earlier this week
